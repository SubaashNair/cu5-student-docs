D14 · PCA, NMF and anomaly detection

Unit: DS 1.3 Machine Learning and Deep Learning · Day 14 of 25

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Finding the rows that do not belong. Types of anomaly, z-score and IQR as the statistical baseline (DS 1.2 D06), Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor and one-class SVM, with the flagged sets compared.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Apply Isolation Forest and LOF with a stated contamination and compare the flags.
  2. Say what each method looks for (global isolation versus local density).
  3. Decide what to do with a flagged row before removing it.
Why this matters

Fraud, faults and data-entry errors. The multivariate methods here find what the per-feature IQR rule cannot.
