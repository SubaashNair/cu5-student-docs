D12 · Filtering, sorting and groupby

Unit: DS 1.1 Python Fundamentals · Day 12 of 14

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Reshaping a table to answer a question. Selecting with loc and iloc, filtering with boolean conditions combined with & and |, sorting by one or several keys, finding and handling missing values (isna, dropna, fillna), groupby with aggregation, and merging two tables with a check on the row count afterwards.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Select rows and columns with loc and iloc and say when each is the right tool.
  2. Filter with two conditions combined and predict the number of rows returned.
  3. Sort by two keys in different directions.
  4. Count missing values per column and choose between dropping and filling.
  5. Group by a category and compute several aggregates in one call.
  6. Merge two tables and explain any change in row count.
Why this matters

Most of an analyst's day is this lesson. The Chipotle assessment is entirely filtering, grouping and a per-order rate; DS 1.2 D04 to D07 assume all of it is fluent, and the merge row-count check is the habit that catches duplicated keys.
