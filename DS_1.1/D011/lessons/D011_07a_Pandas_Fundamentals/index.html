What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

The DataFrame. Series and DataFrame as the two objects, building them from dictionaries and lists, loading a CSV, inspecting with head, info, describe, shape and dtypes, the index, selecting a column and computing its statistics. The lesson keeps pointing at the array and the dictionary underneath.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Build a DataFrame from a dictionary of columns and from a CSV file.
  2. Inspect a new dataset with head, info and describe and report row count, types and missing values.
  3. Explain the relationship between a Series, a DataFrame column and a NumPy array.
  4. Select one column and compute its mean, and select two columns as a DataFrame.
Why this matters

This is the working object of the rest of the credential. Every EDA in DS 1.2 starts with info and describe, and every model in DS 1.3 is fed from a DataFrame.
