D13 · Time series and joining data

Unit: DS 1.1 Python Fundamentals · Day 13 of 14

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Time as an index. Timestamps and DatetimeIndex, to_datetime with formats, date ranges, slicing by date, resampling to a coarser frequency with the right aggregation (size for counts, sum for totals, mean for rates), rolling windows and shifting for change over time.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Parse dates with to_datetime, including a non-default format, and set them as the index.
  2. Slice a time-indexed frame by a date range.
  3. Resample daily data to weekly or monthly and choose the aggregation that answers the question.
  4. Compute a rolling mean and explain what the window does to noise.
  5. State what resample('D').size() counts and why it differs from sum().
Why this matters

Sales by month, daily rides, sensor readings and website traffic are all this. The taxi resample built here is the exact first step of the ARIMA paper on DS 1.2 D21.
