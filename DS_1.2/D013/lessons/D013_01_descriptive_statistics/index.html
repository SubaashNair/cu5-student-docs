D13 · Descriptive statistics, probability and distributions

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 13 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Describing a column honestly. Numerical versus categorical data and why the distinction decides every later method, centre and spread (mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation, quartiles, IQR), skew and which centre to trust, distributions and probability, and the law of large numbers demonstrated by simulation.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Tell numerical from categorical data and name a method each one rules out.
  2. Summarise centre and spread and say which statistic to trust when the data is skewed.
  3. Read skew from a histogram and from the mean-median gap.
  4. Compute quantiles and use them to describe a distribution.
  5. Demonstrate the law of large numbers with a simulation.
Why this matters

Every effect size on D15 and every assumption check on D16 is one of these numbers. The mean-versus-median trap returns on D18, where it decides which test to trust.
