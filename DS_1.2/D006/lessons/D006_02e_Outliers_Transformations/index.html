D06 · Categorical data, encoding and outliers

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 6 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Values that do not belong, and shapes that fight the method. Visual detection with box plots and scatter, statistical detection with IQR and z-scores, the three handling choices (keep, cap, remove) each with a reason, log and Box-Cox transformations for skewed data, and checking skew before and after.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Detect outliers with the IQR rule and with z-scores and compare what each flags.
  2. Decide to keep, cap or remove an outlier and write the reason.
  3. Transform a skewed column with a log and report the skew before and after.
  4. Say which later methods are sensitive to outliers and which are not.
Why this matters

Outliers drive regression fits (the fish and HR incomes on D19 and D20) and break the t-test on D18. The log transform reappears in every model that meets money or counts.
