D02 · Seaborn: distribution and categorical plots

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 2 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Statistical plots in one call. Distribution plots (histogram with KDE, box, violin), categorical plots (count, bar, swarm, strip), relationship plots (scatter, regression line, heatmap), the hue argument for a third variable, and multi-plot grids (FacetGrid, pairplot). Each plot is paired with what it lets you read: spread, skew, outliers, group differences.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Compare a distribution across categories with box and violin plots and read spread and skew.
  2. Add a third variable with hue and say what it reveals.
  3. Choose between a histogram and a KDE and explain what each hides.
  4. Facet a plot by a category and compare panels.
  5. Read a correlation heatmap and pick the pair worth a scatter plot.
Why this matters

The assumption checks of D15 to D18 (normality, equal spread, outliers) are read from these plots before any test is run. The EDA days D04 to D07 use them on every column.
