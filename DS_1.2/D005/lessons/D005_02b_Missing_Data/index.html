D05 · Missing data and time patterns

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 5 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Gaps in data as information. The three missingness types (MCAR, MAR, MNAR) and why the type decides the fix, visualising where values are missing, deletion, simple imputation with mean, median and mode, KNN imputation, and validating the result by comparing the distribution before and after.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Classify a missingness pattern as MCAR, MAR or MNAR from the evidence.
  2. Visualise where values are missing and which columns go missing together.
  3. Choose between deletion and imputation and justify the choice for the type.
  4. Impute with median, mode and KNN and compare the distribution before and after.
Why this matters

Imputation done wrong biases every model in DS 1.3 and can leak the target. Every real dataset has gaps, and the interviewer's question is always 'what did you do with the missing values, and why'.
