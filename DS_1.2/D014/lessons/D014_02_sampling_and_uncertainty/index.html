D14 · Sampling, bootstrapping and confidence intervals

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 14 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

How wrong a sample is. Simple random, weighted and cluster sampling with pandas, sampling bias made visible by plotting a convenience sample against a random one, relative error against sample size, the bootstrap, the standard error, and a confidence interval and what it does and does not promise.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Draw simple random, weighted and cluster samples and say when each is honest.
  2. Show sampling bias by putting two samples on the same axes.
  3. Compute relative error at several sample sizes and describe the curve.
  4. Bootstrap a statistic and read a standard error from the result.
  5. Build a 95 percent confidence interval and state what it means in one sentence.
Why this matters

Every A/B test, survey and quality sample rests on this. The standard error built here is the denominator of every test statistic on D15.
