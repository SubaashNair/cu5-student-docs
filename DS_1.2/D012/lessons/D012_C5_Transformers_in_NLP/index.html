D12 · Sentiment analysis: lexicons, ML and transformers

Unit: DS 1.2 Data Preparation & Statistical Modelling · Day 12 of 21

What to do today

Read this brief, then download and run the lesson notebook from Downloads.

Concept taught

Pretrained language models. Why transformers replaced recurrent networks, the idea of self-attention, and using a Hugging Face pipeline for sentiment and other tasks without training anything.

By the end of this lesson you can
  1. Run a pretrained sentiment pipeline on the same reviews used for VADER and compare.
  2. Explain self-attention in one paragraph without equations.
  3. State the cost and the limits of a pretrained model for a small business task.
Why this matters

Most production NLP today is a pretrained model behind an API. The student learns what it does well and when the simpler D12 methods are enough.
